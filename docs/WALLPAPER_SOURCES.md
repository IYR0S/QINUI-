# Wallpaper sources and reuse notes

Checked on 2026-11-06.

QINS currently draws its built-in theme wallpapers with Canvas. No third-party wallpaper image is bundled by this change. These individual images are candidate references if a later release adds a small, attributed set of optional theme backgrounds.

## Samsung / Galaxy-inspired silver

- [Abstract swirling blue and silver metallic texture — Logan Voss, Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/abstract-swirling-blue-and-silver-metallic-texture--vZxtP873Oo). The image page marks it free under the Unsplash License. Its chrome/liquid-metal treatment is a strong candidate for the silver Galaxy theme.
- [Abstract brushed metal texture with blue-green gradient — Logan Voss, Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/abstract-brushed-metal-texture-with-blue-green-gradient--pbZs_M49bk). Also marked free under the Unsplash License. A subtle cool accent could work as a second Galaxy background.

## License and asset policy

- [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) permits free use and distribution, including commercial use, but does not permit compiling Unsplash images to replicate a similar or competing service. Preserve the photographer/source record with any bundled image.
- [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/legal-pages/license/) permits many uses, including use in an app, but prohibits redistributing its photos/videos on other stock or wallpaper platforms. Pexels' API terms also specifically restrict wallpaper-app replication. For QINS, treat Pexels as a source for user-selected/personal backgrounds only, not as an image pack to redistribute.
- [Pixabay Content License Summary](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/) permits free use and adaptation but prohibits distributing the content on a standalone basis. Do not bundle unchanged Pixabay images as wallpaper files without reviewing the full license and the specific context.
- Do not copy official Samsung or Google stock wallpapers merely because a download page offers them; verify redistribution rights for the exact asset.
- Keep the built-in QINS artwork original and offline-first. Any later external asset should include creator, source page, download date and license notes, and should be optional so the launcher remains functional without network access.
