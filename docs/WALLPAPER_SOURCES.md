# Wallpaper sources and reuse notes

Checked on 2026-11-06.

QINS currently draws its built-in theme wallpapers with Canvas. The links below are candidate references for future optional image packs; they are not bundled into the application by this change.

## Samsung / Galaxy-inspired silver

- [Abstract swirling blue and silver metallic texture — Logan Voss, Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/abstract-swirling-blue-and-silver-metallic-texture--vZxtP873Oo). The page labels the image as free to use under the Unsplash License. Its liquid-metal appearance is a close fit for a silver Galaxy theme.
- [Abstract brushed metal texture with blue-green gradient — Logan Voss, Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/abstract-brushed-metal-texture-with-blue-green-gradient--pbZs_M49bk). Also presented as free to use under the Unsplash License; useful as a restrained alternative if a small cool-color accent is desired.

## Pixel / Material-inspired color

- [Pexels wallpaper collection](https://www.pexels.com/discover/wallpapers/). Pexels states that its images are free to use for personal and commercial projects under the Pexels License. Search for layered mint/teal forms, soft geometric gradients and organic abstract shapes.
- [Pixabay license FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/). Pixabay states that its media is free for commercial and non-commercial use, while warning that depicted trademarks, people and other third-party rights may still apply.

## Asset policy for QINS

- Prefer the current original Canvas artwork for built-in themes unless a downloaded asset is clearly more useful on the QINS1+ screen.
- Do not copy official Samsung or Google stock wallpapers merely because a download page labels them “free”; verify redistribution rights for the specific file.
- For every image eventually bundled, record the original creator, exact source page, download date and license in the repository. Keep the image optional so the launcher remains usable offline.
