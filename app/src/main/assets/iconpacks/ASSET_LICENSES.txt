QINS UI Studio – Original icon artwork

The three PNG atlases in this directory contain original geometric icon artwork drawn specifically for QINS UI Studio. They are not extracted from Samsung, Google, Nothing, or third-party icon packs and do not reproduce proprietary logos.

License: CC0 1.0 Universal for the original PNG artwork. You may copy, modify, and redistribute it.

The packs are inspired only by broad visual language:
- one-ui: rounded color tiles and clear white glyphs
- pixel: soft tonal surfaces and dark glyphs
- nothing-os: monochrome dark tiles with restrained red accents

No brand endorsement is implied. The appfilter files map common Android package IDs to generic functional symbols; unmapped apps retain their installed icons.
